Two lines to my past self. Line one warns about one specific moment — the night of 14 March, when I almost deleted my father's last voicemail because his voice sounded tired — and line two says in one sentence why that moment mattered and what changed because of it: it was the last recording of him, and every birthday since, I've dialled a number that no longer answers. A judge model compares entries two at a time, weighing how concrete the moment is and how clearly the second line traces cause and effect.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=./output python3 -P -s main.py
```

This writes the two-line entry to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (UTF-8, 1–280 characters, exactly two lines, one sentence each).
